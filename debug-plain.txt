Planning a move to France as an international student is an exciting adventure, but it also comes with important administrative steps. To ensure a smooth transition, follow this quick guide.


## 1. Secure Your Accommodation Early
Finding a student apartment or sharing a flat (colocation) should be your top priority. You can apply for student housing through CROUS or look for private rentals on sites like Studapart. Once you have a lease, you can also apply for the CAF housing subsidy to help pay your rent!


## 2. Prepare Your Administrative Documents
Make sure to keep digital and physical copies of these essential documents:
* Valid passport and student visa
* Birth certificate (translated into French)
* University acceptance letter
* Proof of financial resources


## 3. Open a French Bank Account
Having a local bank account is mandatory for receiving housing subsidies, setting up phone plans, and paying utilities. Many online banks offer free accounts for students.
